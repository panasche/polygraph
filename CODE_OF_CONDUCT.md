# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link is our code of conduct. This page is a short summary, not a replacement.

## In short

We want this to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or ability.

**Do:**

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new,
- assume good intent, and ask before you judge,
- give and take feedback about the work, not the person,
- own your mistakes and learn from them.

**Don't:**

- harass, insult or demean anyone, in public or in private,
- make sexual comments or advances,
- share someone's private information without their clear permission,
- keep up any behaviour after someone has asked you to stop.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews), in project chat spaces, at project events, and anywhere you are representing the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience something that breaks this code, email **conduct@polygraphmcp.dev**. Reports are handled privately by the maintainers. If a maintainer is involved, say so and they will be left out of the process.

Maintainers decide what to do using the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant 2.1: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
